How It Works

From Clinical Note to Paid Claim — Seamlessly

AMORA's intelligent pipeline handles the complexity of modern billing so your staff focuses on exceptions, not routine tasks.

1

Claim Intake & Scrubbing — Documentation is ingested and run through 3,000+ edit rules before submission.

2

AI-Assisted Code Review — Our engine cross-references clinical notes to suggest and validate the correct codes.

3

Payer-Specific Routing — Claims are formatted and routed based on each payer's current requirements.

4

Payment Posting & Variance Analysis — ERAs are auto-posted and underpayments are flagged for recovery.
